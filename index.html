01
Phase One

Discover

Six C-Suite agents and their specialist teams connect to your systems, learn your business, and ask the right questions at the right time.

Before anything is built, before any advice is given, the Discovery Engine does something most consultants don't — it shuts up and listens. Six specialised C-Suite agents and their supporting teams connect to every system in your business with read-only access. They map the full picture before saying a word.

Then — once it has enough context — the system asks questions. Not generic onboarding questionnaires. Specific, informed questions that show it already understands your business. Questions that fill in the gaps between what the data shows and what Dickon knows.

Step 1🔌

Connect

Read-only API connections established to POS, Shopify, Xero, email, and social accounts. Zero risk to your systems.

Step 2👁️

Observe

Agents silently ingest data across all four channels. Sales patterns, customer behaviour, financial flows, inventory levels, supplier timings. Building a complete data model of your business as it actually operates.

Step 3🔍

Analyse

Cross-channel pattern recognition. Which retail customers are also online buyers, which wholesale accounts are growing vs stalling, where production misaligns with demand, which content drives real sales vs vanity metrics.

Step 4💬

Ask

Now the system asks Dickon questions. It doesn't ask "what's your biggest challenge?" It says "your Ethiopian single-origin margin has dropped 12% over three months — is that a supplier issue or a pricing decision?"

Step 5📋

Report

The Discovery Report: complete picture across all four channels, identified bottlenecks, missed revenue opportunities, efficiency gaps, and a recommended deployment plan.

The Kind of Questions It Asks

Not right away. After it already knows the answers to the obvious ones.

CFO Agent

"Your House Blend wholesale margin is 18% — below your other blends at 24-28%. Is that intentional volume pricing or has the cost base shifted?"

COO Agent

"Thursday's roast schedule shows 6 hours of idle time between batches. Is this an equipment constraint or scheduling choice?"

CMO Agent

"Your Instagram behind-the-scenes content gets 3x the engagement of product shots, but you post 4x more product shots. Is that a resource constraint?"

CEO Agent

"12 retail customers buy 2+ bags weekly but aren't on subscriptions. Is there a reason you haven't pushed subscription conversion in-store?"

COO Agent

"Three wholesale accounts haven't reordered in 4+ weeks despite steady previous patterns. Are you aware of any relationship issues?"

CFO Agent

"Your DTC shipping costs are 22% of order value on small orders. Have you considered a minimum order threshold or flat-rate box?"

The Zero-Destruction Promise

Every Discovery connection is read-only. The agents observe — they never interfere. Your systems, data, and operations remain completely untouched. Not a single record is modified, created, or deleted during Discovery.

Intelligence

The system stops just reporting and starts thinking. Proactively.

The C-Suite agents run scheduled deep intelligence sessions across every data source — informed by always-on data awareness that tracks your business between sessions. Looking for ways to save money, make more money, and run more efficiently. They don't wait to be asked.

Supplier price drift. Roast schedule waste. Overstaffed shifts. The CFO and COO agents constantly look for places where money is leaking.

Phase Four

Operations

The intelligence layer stops advising and starts doing — pay invoices, send emails, update products, reorder stock. All via the approval queue.

Phase 3 spots the opportunities. Phase 4 acts on them — without Dickon having to log into a single piece of software. The system connects directly to Xero, Shopify, Klaviyo, and your other tools with write access. It prepares every action, puts it in the approval queue, and executes the moment it's approved.

The breakthrough isn't the intelligence. It's the execution. Every proactive recommendation and every requested task routes through a single approval gate — one tap and it's done. In the right system. No logins. No copy-paste. No follow-up.

Stage 1Suggest

Proactive Recommendations

The system spots a cost saving, a revenue opportunity, or a churn risk — and proposes a specific action with full context. Nothing happens until you approve.

Stage 2Execute

Direct Action via the Approval Queue

Pay a supplier invoice in Xero. Send a re-engagement campaign in Klaviyo. Update a product price in Shopify. Raise a purchase order. The system prepares it — one tap executes it. No logins required.

Stage 3Request

Ask It to Do Things in Slack

"Pay the Brazil invoice." "Send our three at-risk subscribers a win-back offer." "Update the House Blend price to £14.50." The system prepares the action, routes to the queue, executes on approval. No dashboards. No SaaS tabs.

OngoingEvolving

Smarter Over Time

Every approved and rejected action teaches the system what matters and what doesn't. Recommendations get sharper. Execution gets faster. The intelligence compounds.
